# Exemplar: ENGR 209 lab technical memo (shape only)

This follows the lab handout's stated memo rules, with every piece of substance replaced by a placeholder. The one completed memo in the archive left out the title-page abstract and the figure captions. This shape follows the handout, not that memo. The course's own Technical Memo instructions were not retained, so if the student has them, they take precedence over this. See `methods.md`.
