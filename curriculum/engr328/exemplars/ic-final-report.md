# Exemplar: IC lab final report (shape only)

- **id:** `ic-final-report`
- **family:** `ic-lab`
- **stage:** `final-report`

This skeleton was paraphrased from the structure of a real final report. **No real values appear here.** The archived submission numbered its title page and ran to about six body pages. **Both are departures from the written rules. Do not copy them**: the title page is unnumbered, and the body is 4 pages.

---

## Title page (unnumbered)

Descriptive title naming the engine type and the two aims (performance and energy analysis) · [Student Name] · ENGR 328, Section [X] · [Instructor] · [Month Year]

**Executive summary.** Operating range tested; peak power and peak torque, each with its speed; peak overall thermal efficiency, with its power and speed; the actual air-fuel ratio against theoretical and the unburned fraction; the exhaust cp comparison; and the split of the chemical-energy drop into shaft power and heat loss. All as numbers with units.

## Body: 4 pages, numbered from 1

| Heading | Content |
|---|---|
| Objective | The three aims: performance curves, efficiency against power, and the energy balance at one point |
| Methods | The apparatus as one schematic figure; shaft power, efficiency, flows, combustion, the calorimeter, and the energy balance as numbered inline equations |
| Results: Performance curves | One table of all operating points (speed, torque, power, fuel flow, efficiency), ordered consistently; one figure of power and torque against speed |
| Results: Efficiency | One figure of efficiency against shaft power, ordered by power, with the trend with load discussed |
| Results: Energy balance | One table of streams at the energy-data point, and one sentence on each stream's share |
| Conclusions | A brief conclusion on each energy stream, then overall conclusions on energies, efficiencies, and reasonableness |

If the body will not fit in 4 pages, cut prose before cutting figure legibility. Move per-point detail to the appendices.

## Lettered appendices

- **A.** List of appendices
- **B.** Raw data: B.1 engine and calorimeter readings for every point, B.2 timed fuel measurements, B.3 per-stream detail at the energy point
- **C.** EES code and solution output

## What changed from the pre-submissions

- Three separate analyses become one argument: peak performance, then efficiency, then where the fuel's energy goes.
- Earlier unit and sign fixes are carried into every table.
- The overall-reasonableness conclusion is new at this stage.
