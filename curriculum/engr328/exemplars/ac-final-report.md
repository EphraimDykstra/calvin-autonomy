# Exemplar: A/C lab final report (shape only)

- **id:** `ac-final-report`
- **family:** `ac-lab`
- **stage:** `final-report`

This skeleton was paraphrased from the structure of a real final report. **All numbers are invented placeholders.** The one submission in the archive ran past the four-page body limit. **Do not copy its length**; the budget below is the one the course states.

---

## Title page (unnumbered)

Descriptive title · [Student Name] · ENGR 328, Section [X] · [Instructor] · [Month Year]

**Executive summary**, about 6-8 sentences:

1. What was balanced: three duct sections, two seasons, forcing against response.
2. The energy error range across all section-season cases, as numbers, naming the best and worst cases.
3. The water error range, the same way.
4. One sentence on the dominant error source.
5. COP and compressor isentropic efficiency for each season, as numbers.

## Body: 4 pages, numbered from 1

| Heading | Content | Rough share of the body |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | 2-3 sentences | small |
| Methods | State fixing, dry-air flow, the forcing/response balance and error definitions, refrigeration assumptions (saturated liquid at the condenser outlet, isenthalpic valve), and the COP and isentropic-efficiency equations, all numbered inline. One figure with the three section control volumes side by side. | about 1 page |
| Results: Section A-B | A few sentences and one table: forcing, response, and error, with both control-volume formulations for summer | about ⅔ page |
| Results: Section B-C | A few sentences and one table | about ½ page |
| Results: Section C-D | A few sentences and one table | about ½ page |
| Refrigeration cycle | One table of COP and isentropic efficiency per season; the P-h and psychrometric charts either here or in an appendix, referenced by number | about ½ page |
| Summary figure | Optional: one grayscale-safe chart comparing forcing and response magnitudes across all six section-season cases, with a takeaway caption and no chart title | about ⅓ page |
| Conclusions | One or two sentences per section pointing to its appendix, then overall conclusions: error size, plausibility, sources, and how to reduce them | about ½ page |

Tables are captioned above, figures below, and every table and figure is numbered in order of first reference.

## Lettered appendices

- **A.** List of appendices
- **B.** Raw data sheet
- **C.** Calculated moist-air properties at stations A-D, both seasons (one table, units in the headings, sensible significant digits)
- **D.** Psychrometric chart and R134a P-h diagram for each season, each with a takeaway caption
- **E.** Equipment and manufacturer data used in the calculations
- **F.** EES code with solution output: F.1 winter, F.2 summer

## What changed from the pre-submissions

- Each section's two to three pages shrink to a paragraph and a table.
- System-level content is new: both charts for both seasons, COP, and isentropic efficiency, plus effectiveness and UA if attempted.
- Earlier errors found during the semester are corrected in the tables and in the EES.
- Conclusions move from per-section to whole-experiment.
