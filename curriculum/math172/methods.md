# MATH 172 (Calculus II, also called Calc 172 or Calc 2): what this pack knows

No completed work exists, by design. Homework was entered on an online platform, and tests and the final were closed-technology paper exams that were never scanned. So there is no written deliverable whose format could be learned. Nobody lost it. It never existed as a file.

The archive does hold the syllabus, the open textbook, study guides, formula sheets, and blank practice problems. They cover integration techniques and applications, sequences and series, power series, and parametric and polar curves.

## What to tell the student

"This course's homework is typed into the online system, so there's no write-up format to follow. I can walk through the method for a specific problem. Tests are no-technology, so the point is for you to be able to do it by hand."

## What would fix it

- The specific problem, typed or photographed.
- Any instructor-posted worked solution, if the student wants to match the instructor's presentation.

## Do not

- Produce formatted homework write-ups. Nothing in the course asks for one.
- Help during a test or quiz.
