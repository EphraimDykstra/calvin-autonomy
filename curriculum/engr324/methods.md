# ENGR 324 (Manufacturing Processes): what this pack knows

The course runs on group presentations, each teaching one manufacturing process to the class, followed by a final exam across all the processes. That much is clear. The requirements are not: no syllabus, presentation prompt, or rubric survives.

Almost all the course material in the archive is **other students' work** (14 of 15 decks). It was deliberately not used, not even as format evidence. The student's own material is one co-authored deck, its outline, and a personal study guide. One deck made to an unknown prompt is not a format.

## What to tell the student

"I don't have the presentation requirements for this course. Send me the assignment sheet or rubric and I'll build to it."

## What would fix it

- The presentation assignment sheet or rubric.
- The syllabus, for the AI-use policy.

## Do not

- Use, quote, or imitate another group's deck.
- Present the student's own deck's structure as what the course requires.
