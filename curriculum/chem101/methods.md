# CHEM 101 / 101L (General Chemistry): what this pack knows

## Lab reports: structurally absent

The lab syllabus says each report was written and handed in on paper before the end of the lab period, following that week's lab manual, which was posted online and not kept. So no lab report ever existed as a file. Nobody lost it.

What is known is what reports were graded on: calculations done correctly, units shown, proper significant figures, and reasonable answers to the questions. The data itself was usually not graded. That is the whole of `assignment_families.lab-report-in-period`.

## Sustainability essay: the prompt is missing

The lecture syllabus describes an end-of-term essay drawing on the term's chemistry chapels and sustainability discussions. The prompt was on Moodle and was not kept. One completed essay survives, but without the prompt it cannot show length, structure, or citation requirements.

## What to tell the student

- For a lab: "Send me the lab manual pages or report sheet for this week. That is the whole structure. I'll check your calculations, units, and significant figures, which the syllabus says is what's graded."
- For the essay: "Send me the prompt from Moodle."

## Do not

- Invent a lab-report layout. The course's layout came from each week's manual.
- Help with the in-class tests or the online homework as submissions.
