# CORE 100: what this pack knows

The archive holds the course's assigned readings and two completed pieces of writing (a structured-dialogue response and a final essay), but no syllabus, no prompt, and no rubric. The course's writing tasks are known to exist. Their requirements are not.

## What to tell the student

"I don't have the prompt or rubric for this course, so I can't tell you the required length, structure, or citation style. Send me the prompt and I'll work from it."

## What would fix it

- The prompt for the task in hand, including length and citation style.
- The syllabus, for the AI-use policy.
- The readings the prompt names, if they are not already at hand.

## Do not

- Use the two surviving pieces as models. Without their prompts they show only that the tasks existed.
- Quote or paraphrase a classmate's position from the dialogue response.
