# "Technical memo" as a short report: thermo-fluids family shape

Shape only. This is what ENGR 209 lab, ENGR 319 and ENGR 328 mean by a technical memo. It is **not** a To/From memo. All numbers are invented stand-ins for an imaginary bench pump test; they are not results from any real lab.

---

**Title page (unnumbered, not counted toward the page limit)**

- Descriptive title: *Efficiency of a Bench-Top Centrifugal Pump Across Its Speed Range*
- [Student Name], course and section, group if the course has one, [Instructor], date

- **Abstract** (on the title page): one or two sentences on what was done and how, then the key results **as numbers with units**. For example: "Peak overall efficiency was 0.47 at 2600 rpm; flow rate scaled linearly with speed within 4 %." Do not write "key results are presented."

**Body, about two pages** (page 1 is the first body page). Figures count toward the limit.

- **Objective**: one short paragraph.
- **Methods**: say what was measured and how, in a few sentences, with a labelled schematic (Figure 1, caption below). Key equations are typed, numbered and explained where they are used.
- **Results**: figures and tables, each cited by number.

  Table 1. Measured shaft power against power computed from the flow model (caption above).

  | Speed (rpm) | Measured (W) | Model (W) | Difference (%) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1800 | 41.2 | 39.6 | 3.9 |
  | 2600 | 88.5 | 84.1 | 5.0 |

- **Discussion**: why the model reads low, which approximation is responsible, and whether a difference of about 5 % changes the conclusion. No plus-or-minus propagation unless the handout asks for it.
- **Conclusions**: one answer per objective, with numbers and units.

**Appendices** (lettered in this family, opening with a list of appendices)

- A. List of Appendices
- B. Raw data sheet (scanned)
- C. Sample calculation, one speed, with units at each step
- D. Spreadsheet or EES output, trimmed and readable

---

Voice for this family: no I, we, you, "the team" or "the author". Spacing is 1.5 unless the handout says otherwise.
