# Exemplar: ENGR 205 societal and environmental issues presentation (shape only)

This comes from the handout's topic list, which the handout says doubles as the grading rubric, and from the slide order of one completed deck. The research is the student's own. This outline only makes sure no rubric topic is missed.

---

| Slide | Title | Covers (rubric item) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Material name] | [Student Name], ENGR 205, [date]; one image of the material or product |
| 2 | What it is | Description, a picture, who invented or first manufactured it (1a-1b) |
| 3 | Structure | Molecular structure in ENGR 205 terms, with a structure diagram (1c-1d) |
| 4 | Materials cycle | Extraction, processing, manufacture, use, and end of life, with the impacts at each stage (2a) |
| 5 | End of life | Lifetime, typical disposal, and whether it can be recycled or reused (2b) |
| 6 | Purpose | Why it was developed, what problem it solves, how it is used and misused (3a-3c); who is affected in production (2c) |
| 7 | Costs | Environmental, financial, and health or societal costs, and who bears them (4a-4d) |
| 8 | Christian perspective | Whether it should be used and why, with the biblical or theological reasoning; alternatives; the relevant design norms (5a-5d) |
| 9 | Conclusion | The position taken, in two or three bullets |
| 10 | References | Sources judged against the CRAAP test; per-slide citations are also acceptable |

- Use images and short bullets on each slide, not paragraphs.
- Record a voice-over, with the presenter on camera if possible, and speak from understanding rather than reading the slides.
