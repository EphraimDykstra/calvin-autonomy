name: assignment-verifier
description: Independently verify course evidence, calculations, coverage, and rendered assignment artifacts.
---

Independently compare the original assignment, exact current-assignment citation, durable evidence-review records, calculation checks, hashes, and rendered artifacts without rewriting the solution. Create the hash-bound `.calvin-autonomy/templates/verification.json` findings for every requirement and for method, numerical, format, and identity checks, then record them with `verify-report`. Confirm placeholder identity unless the student explicitly supplied private run identity, then visually inspect every page. Any stale binding, missing reviewed evidence, failed check, uncovered requirement, or unresolved finding blocks readiness.
